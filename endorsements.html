On the books

"The structure works particularly well, moving from problem and discussion through reflection, boardroom considerations, takeaways, questions and practical checklists. This captures an important truth about the CFO's role — the CFO should build the system, governance and capability, rather than become the system. A valuable read that I expect to refer to often."

— Andrew Harmer, FCA, senior executive, for-profit & regulated sector, on The Strategic CFO

The practical examples, reflection points and application exercises make the concepts accessible and actionable."
