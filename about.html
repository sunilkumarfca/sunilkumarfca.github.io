CA Sunil Kumar, FCA is a Strategic Finance Leader and author who develops practical governance resources for nonprofit organisations, foundations, CSR initiatives and mission-driven institutions.

His publications combine hands-on field experience with structured governance frameworks — helping organisations move beyond compliance toward stronger governance, improved financial sustainability and long-term institutional resilience.

He is the creator of the IGMA™ (Institutional Governance Maturity Assessment) framework — including the flagship IGMA™ Institutional Governance Maturity Toolkit and IGMA™ FCRA Governance Toolkit, both available on Gumroad and Eloquens — used by boards, CFOs and consultants to benchmark governance maturity across the FCRA, ESG, CSR and reserves-sustainability domains. He occasionally offers pro bono governance guidance to select mission-driven organisations — reach out via email or LinkedIn.

All publications are educational and informational resources intended to support governance, finance, compliance and institutional strengthening. They are not a substitute for legal, tax, audit, accounting or professional advice.
