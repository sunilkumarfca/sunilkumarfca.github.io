Sunil is a Chartered Accountant, Strategic CFO and governance leader with 25+ years of experience helping organisations build financial strength, effective governance and long-term resilience — across strategic finance, enterprise risk management, compliance, donor stewardship, institutional sustainability and finance transformation.

He has led large donor-funded programmes for organisations including CRS, The Union, and Population Foundation of India, strengthened governance and internal controls across multiple institutions, and driven ERP, analytics and AI-enabled finance transformation. A career milestone was the successful closure of a USD 60 million donor-funded programme with zero disallowance — a rare outcome that speaks to the rigor of the governance systems behind it.

Why these books and toolkits exist

Frameworks, not theory.

Every book and toolkit here comes out of a real governance problem Sunil has sat with directly — a donor audit, a board that couldn't answer a hard question, a compliance gap discovered too late. The Books distill those experiences into short, readable executive briefings for boards and leadership teams who need the thinking, not a manual. The IGMA™ Toolkits go further: structured, automated Excel assessments that turn "how good is our governance, really?" into a measurable score, with executive dashboards Boards can actually act on. Neither is built to replace legal or audit advice — both exist to help organisations ask better questions, earlier, before a small gap becomes a donor crisis.

He is the creator of the IGMA™ (Institutional Governance Maturity Assessment) framework — including the flagship IGMA™ Institutional Governance Maturity Toolkit and IGMA™ FCRA Governance Toolkit, both available on Gumroad and Eloquens. He occasionally offers pro bono governance guidance to select mission-driven organisations — reach out via email or LinkedIn.

All publications are educational and informational resources intended to support governance, finance, compliance and institutional strengthening. They are not a substitute for legal, tax, audit, accounting or professional advice.
