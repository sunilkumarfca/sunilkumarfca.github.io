About

CA Sunil Kumar, FCA

Chartered Accountant and social-sector CFO with 30+ years in finance, audit and governance. Author of books on NGO governance and strategic finance, and creator of the IGMA™ governance maturity frameworks and toolkits.

Sunil Kumar is a Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA) who has spent more than three decades inside the finance function — first in audit and assurance, then in project controls, and for most of his career in the finance, grants, governance and operations leadership of international development organisations, NGOs, foundations and social-impact institutions in India and South-East Asia. Since 2021 he has held senior and CFO-level finance leadership roles.

That work has been hands-on: managing donor-funded portfolios, reporting to Boards and Audit Committees, handling FCRA and regulatory compliance, building risk and internal-control frameworks, and clearing audit findings. Audit and grant-monitoring assignments took him to every state in India, including remote and hard-to-reach areas — which is why his frameworks are built around how programmes actually run, not only around what the rules require.

His books and IGMA™ toolkits turn that experience into tools any organisation can use on its own. He is the author of The Strategic CFO and The Resilient Institution, among other titles.

What this site offers: books and self-assessment toolkits. Consulting or advisory engagements are not offered through this site.

Where the frameworks come from

IGMA™ and the books are not theory. They come from problems seen repeatedly across three decades of audits, donor reviews and finance leadership.

  • Protect value, create value, report value. Governance and controls protect the institution; good resource allocation creates value; honest reporting connects money to programme results.
  • Anticipate → Strengthen → Grow. Resilient institutions spot regulatory, funding and economic change early, strengthen their systems before they are tested, and grow on that foundation.
  • Evidence over assumption. The question is never just "has this been done?" but "is there a process, a control, evidence and an owner behind it?" — the principle behind every IGMA™ assessment.
  • Built for field reality. Tools designed to work for organisations in remote areas with thin teams, not only for well-staffed head offices.
