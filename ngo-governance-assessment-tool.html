Why most governance reviews miss the real picture

Most governance reviews stop at "do you have a policy?" A policy on file says little about whether it is followed, owned or evidenced. That gap usually surfaces only after a funder audit, a leadership transition or a compliance notice.

IGMA™ asks a different question: what does the evidence support? Each of the 60 questions is broken into Yes/No criteria tied to a maturity level, so the score reflects what the organisation can demonstrate, not what leadership believes. The gap between the two is often the most useful finding.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an assessment take?

It depends on the route. The Concise route suits a single workshop. The Detailed route takes longer because each answer should be backed by evidence; most organisations spread it across a few working sessions with the relevant managers.

Is it only for Indian organisations?

No. The institutional toolkit is designed for NGOs, foundations and trusts in any country. The FCRA and DPDP toolkits are the India-specific ones.

How is the score calculated?

Each question has Yes/No criteria mapped to maturity levels. The workbook scores what the evidence supports on a 1.0–5.0 scale and maps it to L1–L5.
