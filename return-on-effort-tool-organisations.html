Where should our money and effort go?

Every organisation has more good uses for money, staff time and leadership attention than it can fund. This free tool applies the SROE (Strategic Return on Effort) framework to real organisational choices. Should we outsource, hire staff or use a consultant? Should we buy software or add people? Which departments should get the next rupee?

For Boards, CEOs, CFOs and department heads. About 30 minutes for one decision. Answers stay in this browser on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. Deciding for yourself? Use the individual version.

Step 1: Our priorities now

Start with reality. Before choosing how to spend, be clear about what the organisation must achieve this year and where it is weak.

Priority score = Importance × (6 − Strength). Underfunded = importance 4+ and a gap of 2+. Strong = importance 4+ and strength 4+.

Step 2: SROE decision test

Does the complete exchange make sense for the organisation? Look at what each option creates, what it costs over time, what it displaces and what happens if it goes wrong. Keep "do nothing" or "delay" as one option.

Scoring settings (you may change)

Value = Return × + Strategic fit × + Optionality ×

Cost = Effort × + Risk ×

High profile if ratio at least (and fit 3+); Low if below .

Return score = average of the option's three biggest returns. Risk score = average of downside and uncertainty, plus 0.5 if exit is possible only with some cost, or 1 if there is no practical exit (maximum 5). Any option whose downside the organisation could not survive is marked Low. Changing the decision type resets these to that type's suggested settings.

This is a decision aid, not a mathematical answer. It makes the full exchange visible for management and the Board; the judgement stays with you.

Step 3: Fund allocation across functions

Where should the next rupee (or dollar) go? Importance and strength come from Step 1. Add this year's budget for each function, the value of extra funding there, and the risk if it is not funded. Use any one currency.

Funding priority = Importance × (6 − Strength) × (Value of extra funding + Risk if not funded) ÷ 2. Strengthen first = one of the top three by funding priority. Review spend = extra funding adds little value (2 or less) but the function already takes more than its average share of budget. Protect comes first: statutory compliance, safeguarding and donor obligations need their minimum funding whatever the score.
