NGO governance and institutional resilience.

Good governance is what lets a mission-driven organisation keep its promises to donors, regulators, communities and its own staff. Resilience is what lets it keep them through leadership changes, funding shocks, audits and regulatory change.

This section brings the two together, as The Resilient Institution does: how Boards and leadership teams oversee strategy, risk, finance and people; how organisations avoid dependence on a single funder, build reserves and leadership depth; and how governance maturity can be measured with evidence rather than assumed.

Curated by CA Sunil Kumar, FCA — Chartered Accountant and social-sector CFO with 30+ years in NGO finance, audit and governance.
