Why IGMA™

You can't fix a governance risk you can't see.

Most boards find out their governance was weak only after a funder audit, a leadership transition, or a compliance notice forces the question. IGMA™ (Institutional Governance Maturity Assessment) answers it before that happens — scoring where your organisation actually stands, and telling you exactly what to fix first, instead of handing you another policy template to file away.

Common questions

Before you start an assessment.

How is IGMA™ different from a compliance checklist?

A checklist tells you what's missing. IGMA™ scores where your governance actually stands across five maturity levels, and points to the specific decisions your board or CFO should prioritise next.

Do I need to buy an assessment to get value from this site?

No. Start with "Governance Lessons from the Field" — it's free, and carries the same thinking and methodology behind IGMA™, in book form. The rest of the library is priced, but low-cost compared to a full assessment.

Who typically uses the FCRA toolkit versus the Institutional toolkit?

The FCRA toolkit is built for India-registered NGOs managing foreign contribution compliance. The Institutional Governance Maturity toolkit is broader, for any Trust, Foundation or mission-driven organisation reviewing board and financial oversight.

Yes — Gumroad checkout converts to your local currency automatically, and UPI payment is available by email for Indian buyers. See "Good to know before you buy" on the Books and IGMA™ pages.
