FCRA Compliance

10 questions and 50 evidence-based Yes/No statements from the IGMA™ FCRA Compliance Assessment Toolkit v3.0, updated for the FC(R) Amendment Rules 2026: banking, the 20% administrative ceiling, FC-6F, key functionaries, ultimate donors, FC-4 and board oversight, one statement for each maturity level. Answer each statement as it is true today and see an indicative score, a red/amber/green view of each pillar and your five priority areas, with the specific gaps behind them.

About 8–10 minutes · you can stop and come back: your answers stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere · clear my answers

How to answer: You can see your results at any point; unanswered questions are simply left out. YES only if the statement as written is true today and you could show the evidence. NO if it is not true, only partly true or uncertain. N/A if it genuinely does not apply (excluded from the score). Level 1 statements describe a weakness: answer YES if the weakness exists.
