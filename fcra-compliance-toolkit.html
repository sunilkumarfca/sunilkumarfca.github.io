Why a checklist alone is not enough

The FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026 make registration purpose- and State-specific, widen the definition of key functionary, and add ultimate-donor, social-media and detailed activity reporting to Form FC-4. Existing registrants must file Form FC-6F by 21 June 2027.

Most FCRA problems are not a single missing document. They are gaps between Finance, Programmes, HR, Communications and the Board: an activity outside the approved purpose, a State not on the registration, an approval trail that exists only in email. These toolkits assess each requirement for a process, a control, evidence and a named owner, not just "done / not done".
