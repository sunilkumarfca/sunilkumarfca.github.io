Strategic finance and the CFO role in mission-driven organisations.

In many NGOs, Finance is still seen as the team that records and reports what has already happened. A strategic finance function does more: it helps leadership decide where scarce resources should go, protects the organisation against funding shocks, and gives the Board a clear, forward-looking view of risk and sustainability.

This section covers the CFO role in mission-driven organisations, budgeting as strategy, cash flow and restricted funds, financial resilience, and how Finance and Programme teams work together.

Curated by CA Sunil Kumar, FCA — Chartered Accountant and social-sector CFO with 30+ years in NGO finance, audit and governance.
